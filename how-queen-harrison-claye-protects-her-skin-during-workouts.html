How Queen Harrison Claye Protects Her Skin During Workouts

by Beautyfollowup team
published on February 7, 2026

For Olympic hurdler and sprinter Queen Harrison Claye, skin care is just as important as training. Long hours of sweat, sun, and tight hairstyles mean her routine has to be simple, effective, and easy to maintain on the go.

Mid-workout refresh

She keeps a hydrating face mist in her bag to cool down and replenish moisture during training. A quick spritz helps her skin feel fresh and prevents dryness while she pushes through workouts.

Sun protection first

Daily sunscreen is non-negotiable. She looks for lightweight formulas that blend easily, don’t leave residue, and feel breathable during intense sessions. For her, protection should feel invisible — not heavy or greasy.

Hair care matters too

With frequent ponytails, braids, and extensions, deep conditioning is essential. Regular moisture treatments keep her curls strong, soft, and resilient despite constant styling.

Staying active in new ways

Her workouts now mix high-intensity training, cardio, yoga, and dance — often done virtually with family. It keeps fitness fun while maintaining consistency.

Her mindset

Her biggest motivation isn’t competing with others — it’s improving herself. Showing up as her best version each day, whether on the track or in life, is what drives her forward.

Simple rule: hydrate, protect, condition, move, repeat!
